If someone tells you about abuse

WHO’s first-line support approach is called LIVES.[19] You do not need to be an expert. You need to be a safe person to tell.

    Do not pressure anyone to leave, reconcile or report before they are ready. Barangay officials are not allowed to mediate cases under RA 9262, and neither should friends or family push it.[10] See helplines and how to get a protection order.
