Get help

If you are in immediate danger, call 911. You do not need to be sure it is “bad enough” to ask for help, and you do not need an account to use this page.

Who to call

    Numbers are taken from lists published by the Philippine Commission on Women and can change. Confirm current numbers for your region at the PCW helplines page.[10][18]

    Staying safe online

    “Quick exit” in the top bar leaves this site at once. You can also press Esc twice.

    It does not erase your browsing history. If someone checks your phone or computer, open this site in a private window or use a device they cannot access.

    Save evidence such as messages, photos, medical records and dates in a place the other person cannot reach.
